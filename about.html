What We Believe

We are a Southern Baptist congregation. Our beliefs align with the Baptist Faith & Message 2000, the doctrinal statement of the Southern Baptist Convention. The short version:

  • The Bible is God's Word — true, sufficient, and the final authority for what we believe and how we live.
  • God is one — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — eternal, holy, and good.
  • Jesus is God's Son, fully God and fully man, who lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, and rose from the dead.
  • Salvation comes through faith in Jesus alone, by God's grace, not by anything we earn.
  • The church is a community of believers gathered to worship, grow, serve, and share the good news.
  • The Christian life is a daily walk — imperfect, sometimes hard, always lived in dependence on God's grace.

The longer version is in the Baptist Faith & Message 2000. If you want to talk through any of this in person, the pastor is happy to.
