A Word From Our Pastor

"We're glad you're here."

Senior Pastor

Pastor J. L. Franklin

If you've been church-hurt, church-worn, or just plain church-curious — St. James is a place where you can take a breath. We're not here to put on a show. We're here to worship Jesus, love our neighbors, and walk together through what real life actually looks like.

Come Sunday. Sit in the back if you want. Stand in the doorway if you want. We'll save you a seat either way.

— Pastor J. L. Franklin
