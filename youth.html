Youth Ministry

For 6th–12th graders. Wednesday nights, every week. Real teaching, real community, and adults who actually want to be there.

When & Where

Wednesday nights, 6:30–8:00 PM

For Parents

We know what it takes for you to drop your student off here. We want you to know what they're walking into.

What we teach

We teach the Bible. We don't dumb it down. We don't dress it up. We help students wrestle with what Scripture actually says and what it means for who they're becoming.

Who teaches

Our youth ministry is led by {{YOUTH_LEADER_NAMES}}. Every adult who works with our youth has been background-checked and works in pairs — we don't have one-adult-alone-with-students situations, ever.

Permission forms

Annual permission forms are required for retreats, off-site events, and overnight trips. These are digital — we'll send them when needed.

Parent communication

Parents stay in the loop through direct email and text from our youth leaders. Important updates, schedule changes, and event reminders come straight to you — not through your student's phone.

For Students

Come hungry. We usually have food.

Whether you've been coming since the day you could walk or whether you've never set foot in a church before, you're welcome here. We meet every Wednesday at 6:30 PM in {{YOUTH_ROOM_LOCATION}}. Bring a friend. Bring your questions. Bring whatever kind of week you've had.

The Scholarship Program

If you're a graduating senior or already in college and looking for help with tuition — our church's scholarship ministry is open to students from our community.
