Plan Your Visit

Coming to a church for the first time can feel uncertain. We get it. Here's everything you need to know before Sunday.

When & Where

Sunday School — 1:00 AM
Morning Worship — 8:00 AM
Wednesday Bible Study & Prayer Line — 7:00 PM

1105 North 44th St.
Baton Rouge, LA 70802

Get Directions

What to Wear

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see folks in suits and folks in jeans. Nobody's keeping track. Come as you are.

How Long?

Sunday morning worship runs about 75 minutes. Wednesday Bible study is usually closer to an hour.

Bringing kids?

St. James has a dedicated children's ministry during Sunday morning worship. Check-in is in the foyer when you arrive — a volunteer will meet you, walk you to the room, and give you a numbered pickup tag so we know your kids leave with the right person. We take safety seriously.

Older kids and teens are welcome in the main service, or in our Wednesday night youth program for 6th–12th graders.

Parking

Park anywhere you find a spot — there are reserved “guest” spots near the front entrance if you'd like to use them. Someone from our welcome team will be near the door to point you toward the sanctuary.

The Service Itself

Expect singing, prayer, a Scripture-grounded sermon (we're a Baptist church — we believe the Bible matters), and an invitation. No surprises, no awkward “stand up if you're new” moments. We want you to be able to just breathe.

After the Service

If you want to meet someone, the welcome team and the pastor will be near the door. If you'd rather slip out and come back next Sunday with your guard down a little, that works too. There's no pressure.

Want us to know you're coming?

It's not required — you can absolutely just show up. But if it helps your nerves to let us know in advance, drop a line and the pastor will look for you Sunday.
