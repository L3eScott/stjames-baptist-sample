Give Online

Quickly, safely, and securely. One-time or recurring. You choose where your gift goes.

Three ways to give

1. Online (recommended) — the button below opens our secure giving page. Credit card, debit card, or bank transfer.

2. In person — offering plate during Sunday morning worship.

3. By mail — check made out to St. James Baptist Church, sent to 1105 North 44th St., Baton Rouge, LA 70802.

Where your gift goes

  • General Fund — supports the day-to-day ministry of St. James (utilities, staff, maintenance, ministry resources).
  • SJ Youth Ministry — directly funds scholarships for students in our community.
  • Youth Ministry — supports youth events, retreats, and program materials.
  • Outreach & Benevolence — goes to local families and missions partners.

You can designate where your gift goes on the giving page.

Tax-deductible — we'll send you a record.

St. James is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your gift is tax-deductible. We'll send you an annual giving statement in January for the previous year, and a receipt on every online gift.

Questions about giving? Pastor J. L. Franklin or our treasurer can talk you through it — reach out anytime.
